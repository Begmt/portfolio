Source: `generated-avatar.png`, 1280 × 1280, alpha preserved.

## Final prompt

Use case: style-transfer / stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent website guide avatar for Begmat's CRM and systems-architecture portfolio. Image 1 is the style and character reference: friendly rounded 3D toy avatar wearing black over-ear headphones and a muted green shirt with a laptop. Image 2 is ONLY a facial-likeness reference for short dark hair, eyebrow shape, face proportions and warm expression. Create ONE polished stylized character, not a photograph. Preserve the appealing soft clay-like 3D style of image 1. Make the person recognizable from image 2 in simplified cartoon form, with open attentive eyes and a subtle welcoming smile. Waist-up, front-facing, oversized friendly head, shoulders and a small unbranded graphite laptop at the lower edge; one hand makes a small welcoming wave. Keep the head large enough to read as a 48px UI avatar. Dark headphones, muted forest-green shirt, restrained cyan accent matching the portfolio. Soft neutral studio lighting that works on both white and charcoal UI backgrounds. Entire head, headphones and waving hand fully inside the square canvas with a little breathing room. Truly transparent alpha background, no room, no accessories around the character, no framed scene, no floor, no cast shadow outside the cutout, no text, no ASUS or other logos, no watermark. Do not reproduce or include the real photographic portrait anywhere in the output; use it only to inform the cartoon likeness.
